# Research record

Investigation findings for the 2026 Colombian presidential election data.

Every entry states: the claim, how it was measured, the primary artifact it was
measured against, and who verified it. A finding that was relayed from an agent
but not independently re-derived is marked as such and carries less weight than
one measured directly.

Nothing here is a legal conclusion, and nothing here asserts fraud or its
absence. Anomaly is not evidence; absent provenance is not alteration.
