## Method and standing caveats

Every numeric claim in this ledger was **independently re-derived from primary artifacts** — the crawl SQLite stores, the raw stored JSON objects under `objects/sha256/`, the scrutiny document indexes, the historical MMV parquets, and live E-14 PDFs — rather than accepted from any prior verification pass. Where a claim survived first re-derivation it was then **adversarially attacked**: re-computed by a second path that shares no code with the first (raw upstream bytes instead of `normalized_json`; pure SQL instead of Python; a different join key), probed for duplicate rows, silent NULL-skipping, survivorship from parse failures, circular denominators, and grain-mixing, and in one case validated with an injected-defect poison pill to prove the detector could fail at all.

Three standing caveats apply to the entire document:

1. **Nothing here asserts electoral fraud, and nothing here asserts its absence.** Every entry is a structural or arithmetic fact about published artifacts. Several results that read as dramatic — the exterior's share of the second-round margin, the absence of E-14 images for consular mesas — have mundane structural explanations that are themselves documented below. Readers who take any line here as evidence of manipulation, or as a clean bill of health, are misreading it.

2. **Most 2026 reconciliations are internal-consistency results, not independent audits.** The polling-place and mesa records come from the same endpoint family on the same Registraduría host, and the place record is the server's own aggregate of the mesa records. Exact agreement is the expected outcome of server-side derivation. These results establish **capture integrity** — that the crawl and parser faithfully reproduce what was published — and nothing more.

3. **The 2026 figures are the PRECONTEO (preliminary count), not the legal result.** Every 2026 record carries `provenance.legal_status = "preliminary"` and `source_type = "pre_count"`. The round-2 snapshot is frozen at 2026-06-21 ~21:34 (`mdhm` = `06212134` national, `06212208` mesas). The official escrutinio closed four days later. The 2018/2022 comparators are MMV mesa-annex data, a different stage of the count. Any longitudinal comparison must disclose this.

4. **The live precount host is unreachable from this environment.** `resultadosprecpresidente2026-1v` and `-2v.registraduria.gov.co` return `curl` status 000 (connection timeout, host-level, not transient). All 2026 precount results rest on the stored, hash-verified snapshot captured 2026-08-03/04. The escrutinio hosts do answer (HTTP 200) and were used for E-14 forensics.

**Handling compliance.** E-14 forms carry jurado signatures and cedula numbers. No PDF was written to disk during verification; bytes were held in memory only and reduced to structural facts (hashes, `/ID` hex, filter names, page counts, character counts). No name or cedula appears anywhere in this record. One E-14 acta (`e14_sample.pdf`, 2,093,173 bytes) was found persisted in the shared scratchpad by an earlier session in violation of the handling rule; it was flagged, and at the time of writing it is **no longer present** on disk (verified by directory listing — only public reports remain).

## 2. CORRECTED and REFUTED claims — what was wrong and why

These are the entries that cannot be published in their asserted form. Each gives the defect, the reason it matters, and the sentence that survives.

### 2.1 REFUTED — "`state='observed'` on all fields means no identity is satisfied by an imputed value"

**What was asserted:** that because all count-kind totals carry `state="observed"`, none of the ballot identities can be satisfied by an imputed value.

**Why it fails.** The premise is true and vacuous, and the conclusion is directly disproved.

- `state` is a **constant across the entire store**: all 4,640,235 metric fields, every plan, every grain, both rounds, are `'observed'`. Zero exceptions. The parser emits `unknown` / `unavailable` only when the source literally sends `DESCONOCIDO` / `""` / `N/A`, which never occurred. A field that never varies certifies nothing.
- Direct disproof: at mesa grain, `absten` and `centota` are **identically 0 on all 122,020 mesas in both rounds**, while the national record reports `centota = 41,421,973` and `absten = 17,443,669`. Those are structurally absent values stamped `'observed'`. In R2, all 14,439 polling-place records carry `centota = 0`, also `'observed'`.

**Defensible substitute:** *no total was missing or non-numeric, because `precount.py::_required_metric` rejects such records outright and zero records were rejected (all 122,020 rows per plan are `parse_status='parsed'`; `plans.expected = planned = 122,020`).*

## 4. The three strongest findings

Each is stated as the single sentence that could be published and survive hostile expert review from either political direction. Each is followed by the qualifiers that must travel with it.

### 4.1 The exterior/domestic decomposition of the 2026 second-round margin

> **In the Registraduría's own preliminary count (preconteo) of the 2026 presidential second round, the 3,670 consular mesas of department 88 cast 614,095 of 26,345,364 ballots — 2.33% — and produced a 177,809-vote margin, while the 118,350 domestic mesas produced a 73,021-vote margin; the exterior therefore accounts for 70.89% of the 250,830-vote national margin.**

*Why it holds under attack:* re-derived by four independent routes that agree to the unit — the national JSON, the 122,020 mesa records, the 14,438 polling-place records, and a raw pass over all 122,020 stored objects with every byte-stream verified against `items.source_hash` (0 mismatches, 0 missing, 122,020 distinct object hashes). Both internal arithmetic identities close exactly on every component (390,949 + 213,140 + 8,960 = 613,049; + 902 + 144 = 614,095 for the exterior; 12,568,593 + 12,495,572 + 417,888 = 25,482,053; + 219,861 + 29,355 = 25,731,269 for the domestic side). The exterior is fully reported in both rounds (`metota` = `mesesc` = 3,670).

*Qualifiers that must travel with it:* it is the **preconteo**, not the legal result; 26,345,364 is ballots cast, not registered voters; 70.89% is a share of a **net margin**, an inherently unstable denominator (the national margin is 0.95% of ballots cast), so the robust framing is the disproportion itself — 2.33% of ballots, 70.89% of the margin — driven by the internal margin, 28.95% inside the exterior against 0.28% domestically; blank votes (426,848) exceed the national margin; and **this is an arithmetic decomposition of published figures, not evidence of any irregularity.** A large diaspora skew is a normal and repeatedly observed feature of Colombian presidential elections — it ran +4.72% of the national margin toward the winner in 2018 and −10.31% against the winner in 2022.

### 4.2 The escrutinio E-14 document universe structurally excludes the exterior

> **Both rounds' 2026 escrutinio document indexes cover exactly 33 department codes and contain zero E-14 references for department 88 (CONSULADOS) — the escrutinio channel declares `mesasEscrutadas 118350 / mesasFaltantes 0 / 100.0%` over a universe that is precisely the 122,020 total mesas minus the 3,670 consular ones — and the Registraduría's own 2026 scrutiny handbook enumerates no exterior comisión escrutadora, consular results entering only at CNE national consolidation, so the absence follows from the scrutiny architecture rather than from any observed omission.**

*Why it holds under attack:* established affirmatively, not by omission. The R1 escrutinio crawl requested all 949 department-88 puesto JSONs and every one returned `[]`. The 33-code set is identical in both indexes and reproduces independently from the filename field and from the divipole objects. The commission-tree object lists exactly 33 departmental commissions, all domestic. The arithmetic closes to the digit: summing every mesa row across all R1 per-puesto actas-documentos JSONs gives 118,350 rows, of which 118,343 carry a filename and 7 are blank — exactly the R1 index count. The mechanism is documented in the Registraduría's own handbook (p.14 taxonomy, p.13, p.79) and in Ley 163 de 1994 art. 6 parágrafo.

*Qualifiers that must travel with it:* the escrutinio divipole itself enumerates only 33 departments, so department 88 is outside the channel's declared scope — this is a design fact, not a discovered gap. The "crawled and received `[]`" evidence exists for **round 1 only**; the R2 raw store never requested department 88. The R2 index figure of 118,308 is **not** a pure upstream fact — 39 of its 42-mesa shortfall is one puesto we failed to collect (HTTP 503, quarantined), not an upstream absence. And the 2018 evidence points the other way: in 2018 the E-14 portal's published count exceeded the domestic mesa ceiling by ≥2,395 (R1) and ≥2,417 (R2), so consular actas **were** published that year — whether they were in 2022 is unresolved (§3.1). Anyone arguing that the exterior has "never" had document backing would be wrong.

### 4.3 The exterior's growth outran turnout growth in 2022→2026 and not before

> **Across presidential second rounds, exterior ballots grew 18.0% from 2018 to 2022 and 100.6% from 2022 to 2026, while total ballots grew 16.1% in each interval; the exterior share of ballots was flat at 1.33% and 1.35% before rising to 2.33%, and ballots per exterior mesa rose from 107.3 to 120.0 to 167.3.**

*Why it holds under attack:* all six underlying values re-derive exactly (2,419 / 2,552 / 3,670 mesas; 259,497 / 306,184 / 614,095 ballots), the 2026 figures reproduce at two independent grains and sum exactly to the national record on every field, and the metric is comparable across the year boundary — in all three years, candidates + blank + null + unmarked equals the reported total exactly, so the 2018/2022 MMV category sum and the 2026 `votant` are the same construct. Department 88 is confirmed as the exterior register rather than assumed: 2022 geography names it CONSULADOS with country-named municipalities, and 2018 and 2026 share identical sub-code numbering.

*Qualifiers that must travel with it:* the 2026 points are **pre-count** while the 2018 and 2022 points are the MMV mesa annex — not identical stages of the count. The 2018 exterior figures are short of the official installed universe by 13–17 mesas (§2.12), and the 2022 MMV exterior identities (2,552) exceed the 1,343 census-installed exterior mesas reported at the time — both release-level anomalies that bound this series and are **not** resolved. Mesa count explains only part of the 2022→2026 jump: exterior mesas grew 43.8% while exterior ballots grew 100.6%. Exterior censo per mesa in 2026 runs a median of 600 and a maximum of 694, so the higher ballots-per-mesa figure is internally consistent with a larger registered roll per consular mesa; and 949 exterior polling places in 2026 is a **decrease** from 1,000 in 2022, so the exterior footprint should never be narrated as an expansion of locations. Rapid diaspora-registration growth has many ordinary explanations; **this finding identifies a rate change to be explained, not a conclusion about how it arose.**
